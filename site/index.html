  4. Base-N DEC/HEX/BIN/OCT, and/or/xor via OPTN
  5. Equation polynomials (deg 2–4), 2–3 unknowns
  6. Statistics 1-variable & linear regression, plots
  7. Algorithms animated sorts & searches with op counters
  8. Big-O growth curves, cost at n, empirical fitting, Master theorem, classify T(n)
